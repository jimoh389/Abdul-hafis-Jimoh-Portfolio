About

A PM who thinks like a systems engineer.

Before product management, I spent years leading safety operations on offshore and onshore engineering sites. That experience taught me something most PMs spend years learning: the real cost of ignoring user pain points is failure.

As a Health & Safety Officer, I conducted audits, interviewed teams, identified gaps, prioritised fixes, and measured outcomes — root-cause analysis, stakeholder alignment, and cross-functional execution under pressure. That is, fundamentally, the product management mindset. I didn't transition into PM; I recognised that I had been doing it all along.

Today I combine that operational rigour with hands-on product craft — research, strategy, PRDs, QA leadership, and go-to-market — across products in Nigeria and Ghana. I also use vibe coding and AI-assisted development to build working product pages, including the Trios landing page. I'm targeting B2B SaaS and fintech roles where structured thinking and user-centred design converge.
